# 👤 Cashier — Quick Guide
**tips · Cashier Management** — https://cashier-app-psi.vercel.app

**First login:** email + temporary password → click **🔑 Change Password** and set your own. Keep it private.

## Your daily job: Daily Collections
1. **Daily Collections → + New Collection.** Choose Date, Outlet, and the **Staff** you're collecting from.
2. Enter **System Sales** (what the POS says that staff sold).
3. Enter the money handed in: **Cash, CRDB, Stanbic, M‑PESA**.
   - **Cash Required from Staff** fills automatically = System − (CRDB+Stanbic+M‑PESA). Negative = 🟢 Excess Cash.
4. **🚫 Cancellations** — voided punches: reason (Double/Out of Stock/Wrong Punch) + product + qty.
5. **🧾 Signed Bills** — credit sales this staff served (Admin/Director/Customer/Tips/DJ/Staff Loss).
6. **✅ Paid Bills** — old debts collected today. Use **🔗 Link to signed bill** to apply to the right bill.
7. Check the **Staff Loss** preview, tick **"No other bills"** if none, then **Save**.

## Other screens
- **Signed Bills** — record a credit bill; optionally **itemise products** (Amount = products total).
- **Paid Bills** — record a payment; pick payer (category auto‑fills); it settles their oldest bills first. Click a row for its full **payment story**.
- **Petty Cash** — *(if you're a requester)* fill the **Cash Request Form**.

## Remember
- **Staff Loss = System − Collection − Signed − Paid (Staff‑Loss only).**
- You can only **edit/delete collections for your own outlet**.
- A payment auto‑applies to the **same person + category**, oldest bill first.

*Help or changes → contact the system owner.*
