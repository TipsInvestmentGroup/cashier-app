👤 Cashier

Daily routine

Daily Collections

  • + New Collection → choose Date, Outlet, and the Staff you're collecting from.
  • Enter System Sales (what the POS says that staff sold).
  • Enter money handed in: Cash, CRDB, Stanbic, M‑PESA. Cash Required auto‑fills = System − (CRDB+Stanbic+M‑PESA); negative shows 🟢 Excess Cash.
  • 🚫 Cancellations — voided punches: reason + product + qty.
  • 🧾 Signed Bills — credit sales served (Admin/Director/Customer/Tips/DJ/Staff Loss).
  • ✅ Paid Bills — old debts collected; use 🔗 Link to signed bill to apply to the right one.
  • Check the Staff Loss preview, tick “No other bills” if none, then Save.

Other screens

  • Signed Bills — record a credit bill; optionally itemise products (Amount = products total).
  • Paid Bills — record a payment; payer category auto‑fills; settles oldest bill first. Click a row for its full story.
  • Petty Cash — (if a requester) fill the Cash Request Form.
Staff Loss = System − Collection − Signed − Paid (Staff‑Loss only). You can only edit/delete collections for your own outlet.
🧮 Accountant

Money & control

Everything a cashier can do, plus:

Receivables

Outstanding debts by person — aging, overdue, credit limits. Search + export.

Payroll Deductions

Over‑limit Admin/Director bills + staff losses by month. Run Deduction, Email Directors (also auto‑emails monthly).

Reports (CSV / Excel / PDF)

Financial Summary · Daily Cashier Report · Cash Reconciliation · Digital Payment Reconciliation.

Cash Reconciliation

Opening auto‑fills from yesterday's closing; enter Cash Deposited; closing computes. (Cash Verified is officer‑only.)

Approvals

Approve / Reject Cancellations, Tips & DJ, and Customer bills.

Rejected items are excluded from all financial totals; Pending still counts. Digital: Required = Closing − Opening; Variance = Reported − Required.

👑 Owner / Admin

Full control & setup

Log in with the owner email to get owner powers.

One‑time access setup

  • Users (⚙️) — create/edit/delete accounts, reset passwords (owner only).
  • Persons → 🔐 Manage Access — pick the 3rd person manager.
  • Petty Cash → 🔐 Manage Request Access — who can submit cash requests.
  • Reconciliation → 🔐 Extra verifier — extra verification officer.
  • Departments / Categories / Payment Channels / Products — maintain lists & catalogue.

First‑time production setup (run once)

  • Set Vercel env: strong JWT_SECRET, CRON_SECRET, NEXT_PUBLIC_OWNER_EMAIL, SMTP_*.
  • /api/admin/seed?secret=CRON_SECRET — outlets + persons.
  • /api/admin/setup-team?secret=CRON_SECRET&password=<TEMP> — staff accounts.
  • Share temp passwords privately; everyone changes theirs on first login.
